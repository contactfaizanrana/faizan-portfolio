Frequently Asked Questions

Clarity and transparency on the deployment process.

1. Why operate on US/UK hours from Pakistan? +

My standard shift is 9 PM to 6 AM PKT. This creates a massive operational advantage: you assign a task or campaign build at the end of your workday, and it is completed by the time you wake up.

2. How long until I see results from marketing? +

Infrastructure improvements (site speed, pixel setups) are immediate. Ad campaign optimization generally takes 14 days of data gathering before we scale budgets for maximum ROI.

3. Do you build custom sites or use templates? +

Both, depending on your budget. I specialize in custom HTML/Tailwind builds for speed, as well as highly optimized headless Shopify architectures.

4. What happens on the Free Strategy Call? +

It is a 30-minute technical deep dive. We audit your current digital bottlenecks and determine if my solutions align with your revenue goals. No hard selling.

5. How do you handle account security? +

I operate with strict separated access. I only request the precise permissions needed (e.g., Editor access on Shopify or Meta Ads) to ensure your master data remains secure.

6. What ad platforms do you specialize in? +

Over the last 5 years, I have specialized primarily in Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google Ads, focusing heavily on e-commerce conversion tracking.

7. Do you work with existing Shopify stores? +

Yes. We can audit and optimize an existing store, fixing broken integrations, speeding up load times, and improving the checkout funnel.

8. Who owns the assets you build? +

You do. 100%. Everything built—from ad copy to custom code—is deployed directly onto your owned infrastructure.

9. How is communication handled? +

We maintain asynchronous communication via email or Slack, with weekly or bi-weekly sync calls to review performance metrics.

10. What makes your approach different? +

I am not just a marketer; I am an architect. I don't just send traffic to a bad website. I fix the website, set up the tracking, and then run the ads. It is a complete ecosystem approach.
